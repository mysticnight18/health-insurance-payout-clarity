This is a comparison, not a payout prediction.

The tool only compares the amounts you enter. It cannot interpret policy wording, calculate exclusions, or know what the insurer will ultimately approve.

Before acting: confirm the latest payable estimate with your insurer and ask for unclear deductions or limits in writing.

What it uses

Your typed hospital estimate, insurer estimate, and available funds.

What it doesn’t do

Read a policy, submit a claim, or decide whether an expense is covered.
